Transduction source library

Which L1 (or SVA) produced an insertion? When the insertion carries a piece of unique genomic sequence that sat downstream of its progenitor, the answer can be read off directly. This page documents the library of candidate progenitors (“sources”) that annotate uses for that lookup, how it was built, how a lookup works, and how a source that is not in the library is accepted and added.

1. Transductions in one paragraph each

3' (partnered) transduction. L1's own polyadenylation signal (AATAAA at the very 3' end, overlapping the poly-A) is weak, so a fraction of transcripts read through into the downstream genome and stop at the next usable pA site. Reverse transcription of such a transcript by TPRT inserts L1 sequence (usually 5'-truncated) followed by the unique downstream segment and then the poly-A (Moran et al. 1999; Goodier et al. 2000). The segment identifies the source.

Orphan transduction. The same readthrough transcript, but reverse transcription stops before reaching L1 sequence (extreme 5' truncation): the insertion is only the transduced segment plus poly-A, flanked by a TSD — it looks like a “random” templated insertion unless the segment is recognised as the downstream flank of a source (Tubio et al. 2014). Annotate calls these ORPHAN_TD.

SVA 5' transduction. SVAs can be transcribed from an upstream (external) promoter; the transcript then starts upstream of the SVA hexamer and the insertion carries upstream genomic sequence at its 5' end (Damert et al. 2009, MAST2-driven SVA_F1 group; ~8 % of SVAs). Annotate tags TD5P using flanks_5p_sva.fa.gz. SVAs also make 3' transductions (Xing et al. 2006), so SVA sources have 3' flanks too.

Rebuild: bash tools/rte_library/fetch_inputs.sh then python tools/rte_library/build.py (details and provenance in resources/rte_library/README.md).

Coordinates

Published GRCh37 positions are lifted hg19→hg38→hs1 with the UCSC chains. Only same-chromosome hits are accepted and an element's lift must be bracketed by its ±1 kb anchors, because the over.chain files contain small paralog chains (we saw an SVA on chr6 “lift” onto an SVA on chr1). A source is reference when a young L1 (L1HS/L1PA2–8, ≥4 kb) lies within 300 bp of the published position in hg38; otherwise it is non-reference and the published position is the insertion point. The flank of a non-reference source is ordinary reference sequence — exactly what gets transduced — so it is extracted from hs1 at the lifted junction. 34 reference (hg38) sources are absent from CHM13 and 16 non-reference ones are present in it; both are handled (hs1_status).

Flank length

From the published catalogues (n = 4313 transductions with source and segment coordinates): the transduced segment is short (median 314 bp, 95th percentile 1110 bp), but its distal end — where the readthrough transcript was polyadenylated — lies a median 666 bp and up to 19513 bp downstream of the source's 3' end; 99.3% lie within 10 kb (the window used by TraFiC-mem and MEIGA) and 99.9% within 15 kb, the window used here (Tubio et al. 2014 saw transductions reaching ~12 kb).

Transduction endpoints cluster at the source's pA sites

Zumalave et al. (2024, bioRxiv; long reads) showed that the transductions of one source end at the same place: all 83 transductions of the 2q24.1 source in tumour PD0270a end exactly 234 bp downstream, at an alternative pA site — that source lacks the canonical L1 pA signal — whereas a 1p22.3 source with a strong canonical signal makes mostly solo-L1s and a few transductions ending at an APARENT-predicted downstream site. Two consequences used here: (i) each source row lists the first AATAAA/ATTAAA hexamers of its flank (pas_hexamers_3p) and whether it keeps the canonical 3' AATAAA (canonical_pas_3p); a transduced segment ending 10–35 bp past one of them is extra support; (ii) a source's activity is under-counted by transductions alone when its canonical signal is strong — hotness is a lower bound.

Validation

383 of 400 randomly drawn published transduced segments (Rodriguez-Martin 2020) map onto flanks_3p.fa.gz in sense orientation (1 antisense, 16 unmapped), median offset 316 bp into the flank.

Tubio et al. 2014 Table S3 (checked inside build.py): each of the 637 partnered/orphan transductions with an identified source is lifted hg19→hg38→hs1 and must fall inside the flank window of the source row its Table S5 entry was merged into, downstream in element sense (100 bp slack at the source end; sequence alignment to the flank when the lift fails). Hits: 629/637 (98.7%) overall — partnered 305/312 (97.8%), orphan 324/325 (99.7%). Of the misses, four 45–65 bp 10q25.1 “segments” are the source's own genomic poly-A tail (absent from hs1 with the element); the others are a non-liftable Xq27.2 segment, a strand conflict at 8q24.13 (Tubio vs RepeatMasker), a chain artefact at 15q23 and an Xp22.2 orphan overlapping its source's 3' end. The 15 kb window covers 100.0% of Tubio distal ends (max 11824 bp).

Ta/pre-Ta typing of the 146 L1Base intact L1s (two 3'UTR diagnostic sites, L1.3 5931 = Boissinot et al. 2000's ACA/G site, and 5712) reproduces the long-read subfamily calls of Nam 2023 for 50/50 Ta and 28/29 pre-Ta sources; result: 78 L1HS-Ta and 32 L1HS-pre-Ta.

Polymorphic L1s are positions, not sources

Tubio Table S7 lists 1478 putative polymorphic L1 insertions found by TraFiC in 244 matched normals, with carrier counts — but no strand, no length and no sequence, while most polymorphic L1 insertions are 5'-truncated and inactive. Shipping each with a 15 kb flank would add ~1,500 mostly dead candidate sources (≈ 6 MB) that attract spurious assignments. They are kept as lifted positions in polymorphic_l1_candidates.tsv: 102 are already non-reference or hs1-only sources, 2 are present in CHM13 (length and identity known), 13 sit on an L1 already in hg38 (uninformative) and 1361 carry no length information. Annotate may use them in the novel-source rule at tier B only (section 5).

4. Lookup algorithm (annotate)

  1. Extract the candidate segment. From the insertion consensus (pooled reads, indel-aware): the sequence between the element's 3' end and the poly-A (partnered), or the whole insert before the poly-A when no element sequence is present (orphan). Discard ≤ 20 bp segments (too short to place uniquely; these are handled as TEMPLATED_LOCAL or untemplated).
  2. Known sources. Align to flanks_3p.fa.gz (mappy/minimap2 sr, or bowtie2 --local). Accept a hit if ≥ 30 bp aligned at ≥ 95 % identity, on the forward strand of the flank (flanks are in element sense, so a genuine transduced segment is always forward), outside soft-masked repeat for most of its length, and the poly-A follows the segment's distal end in the insertion. Report TD3P + TD3P_SOURCE=<id>, the flank offsets (start ≈ distance from the source's 3' end; end = transcript termination) and whether the end sits 10–35 bp past a pas_hexamers_3p hexamer. Several equally good sources (segmental duplications) → list all ids separated by |.
  3. Element–source concordance. For a partnered transduction the element part should be the source's class (L1 tag ↔ L1 source). A tag in the flank of an SVA source after L1 sequence is CHIMERIC_ENDS, not a transduction.
  4. Unknown flank → novel source. If nothing in the library matches, map the segment to hs1 (unique, MAPQ ≥ 20). Look upstream of the hit, strand-aware, for a source element: for a hit on + at position p, a + strand L1 whose 3' end lies in [p − 15 kb, p]; for a hit on −, a − strand L1 whose 5'-most base lies in [p, p + 15 kb]. Apply the rule in section 5. Without such an element, check the cohort's own non-reference L1 calls; otherwise the segment is not a transduction (TEMPLATED_LOCAL if it comes from ≤ 15 bp of the insertion site, else UNKNOWN).
  5. SVA 5' transduction. Sequence 5' of an SVA hexamer that aligns forward to flanks_5p_sva.fa.gz → TD5P.

5. Accepting a novel source

Rule (refines the SPEC “Novel source rule”). The transduced segment is unique on hs1 (MAPQ ≥ 20), not in flanks_3p.fa.gz, lies 0–15 kb downstream (strand-aware) of a candidate element, and the insertion carries TPRT hallmarks (poly-A after the segment, TSD or EN motif). The candidate element is scored with common.cons_identity (best of consensus- in-element and element-in-consensus infix identity to the L1HS consensus):

  • Tier A — credible: reference L1 ≥ 5.5 kb (5'UTR promoter present) and identity ≥ 0.98, or a full-length L1 insertion called elsewhere in the same cohort (non-reference; no sequence needed). Report TD3P_SOURCE=novel:<hs1 chr:start-end(strand)>, NOVEL_SOURCE and the identity.
  • Tier B — reasonably similar: ≥ 5.5 kb and 0.95 ≤ identity < 0.98, or no reference element but a polymorphic_l1_candidates.tsv position (Tubio S7, TraFiC polymorphic L1; length/activity unknown) in the strand-aware upstream 15 kb window. Report the same tags (with the identity, so the score can down-weight it); append to the library only with ≥ 2 independent daughters (different insertion sites, or different patients).
  • Not a source: identity < 0.95 or < 5.5 kb.

Why these numbers (identity to our L1HS consensus, 40 random full-length copies per family): L1HS median 0.993 (5th percentile 0.989); L1PA2 median 0.974 (0.963–0.985); L1PA3 median 0.949; L1PA4 0.913; L1PA5 0.891. The 56 published reference sources with ≥ 1 daughter: median 0.995, 5th percentile 0.991, minimum 0.966 (two L1PA2-annotated copies typed pre-Ta by long reads — the reason tier B exists). Retrotransposition-competent L1s are L1HS (Brouha et al. 2003: ~80–100 per genome, a handful hot); the youngest L1PA2 can still transduce when reactivated (Nam 2023 lists 11 PA2 sources).

Adding it to the library

tools/rte_library/add_source.py re-checks the tier, refuses a duplicate (existing source on the same strand with its 3' end within 1 kb), extracts the 15 kb downstream flank from hs1 (soft-masked when the hs1 RepeatMasker track is given), appends one row to transduction_sources.tsv (seed=novel_accepted, notes=NOVEL_SOURCE;tier=…) and one record to flanks_3p.fa.gz (re-bgzipped and re-indexed), and refreshes manifest.tsv. With the inputs fetched by fetch_inputs.sh into /data/rte_inputs:

python tools/rte_library/add_source.py --library resources/rte_library \
    --hs1-2bit /data/rte_inputs/genomes/hs1.2bit \
    --hs1-rmsk /data/rte_inputs/genomes/hs1.repeatMasker.out.gz \
    --chrom chr5 --start 1000001 --end 1006030 --strand + \
    --evidence "PEAR-TREE PD12345: 3 daughters (chr2:..., chr7:..., chr11:...)" --n-daughters 3

For a cohort-called non-reference L1 use --junction <0-based offset> instead of --start/--end. Rebuilding with build.py regenerates the library from the published lists and seeds, so appended sources must be re-applied (keep the add_source commands with the cohort's results).

6. Files

  • transduction_sources.tsv — one row per source: id, class, subfamily (rmsk + long-read call in brackets), Ta status, hg38 band, reference status, hs1 and hg38 coordinates, strand and how it was determined, published hg19 coordinates, L1Base id, identity to L1HS, canonical pA, evidence, seed, daughters (total and per study), hotness, alt ids (e.g. LRE3), cell-culture activity, flank names and pA hexamers, and (last) origin germline/somatic (17 somatic).
  • flanks_3p.fa.gz — element-sense downstream flanks; description hs1:chr:start-end(strand). Sources with unknown strand (19 non-reference loci) have two records, <id>/+ and <id>/-.
  • flanks_5p_sva.fa.gz — 5 kb upstream of each SVA source, ending at its 5' end.
  • polymorphic_l1_candidates.tsv — Tubio 2014 S7 TraFiC polymorphic L1 positions (hg19, hg38, hs1), carrier count, status, and the library source when already present; tier-B input only.
  • active.tsv — 181 L1s regarded as active (all L1HS-class intact L1s + published sources with ≥ 5 daughters) with identity to the consensus; used for nearest_active / element_identity.
